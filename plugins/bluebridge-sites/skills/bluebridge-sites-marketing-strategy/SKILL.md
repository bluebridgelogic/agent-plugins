# Blue Bridge Sites Marketing Strategy

Read [access and capability rules](references/access.md). Select the authorized organization and request `get_organization_context` only with clear awareness of its capability status. If it is mocked, use client context available in the conversation and ask for the smallest missing facts.

Understand business outcomes, audience questions, discovery/referral needs, positioning, current channels and production capacity. Use the client's existing documents or memory when actually available. Distinguish confirmed decisions, proposed actions and unknowns. Recommend priorities with reasons, tradeoffs and what evidence would change them. Search and AI visibility are goals to investigate, not guaranteed outcomes.

Turn agreement into a concise strategy proposal: goals, audience, positioning, channel priorities and next decisions. For concrete content items, use the plugin's bluebridge-sites-content-planning skill if available. If asked to save, check capabilities and authorization first. The current strategy/backlog write previews are non-persisting mocks; clearly label the proposal as conversation-only and offer exportable text rather than promising future retrieval. Social integrations and automated execution are not available yet.
