# Blue Bridge Sites Performance Review

Read [access and capability rules](references/access.md). Select the authorized site and check `get_performance_summary` capability. Current responses are mocks: do not supply sample client metrics or infer measured progress from a successful setup.

When real measurements exist, record source, observation date, date range, coverage and material gaps. Explain traffic, search visibility, AI citations and verified inquiries separately; none alone proves business impact or causation. Compare equivalent periods and identify changes in tracking before drawing conclusions. Use only connected/authorized properties.

Until measurement connections exist, propose a measurement plan: goals, relevant metrics, required properties/access, starting baseline and useful review cadence. Recommend next content/website experiments as hypotheses. Do not claim to connect analytics, schedule recurring reports or save a review when those operations are unavailable. Return an exportable, clearly labeled proposal.
