# Review my marketing results

Read [access and capability rules](references/access.md). Select the authorized site and check `get_performance_summary` capability. Current responses are mocks: do not supply sample client metrics or infer measured progress from a successful setup.

When real measurements exist, record source, observation date, date range, coverage and material gaps. Explain traffic, search visibility, AI citations and verified inquiries separately; none alone proves business impact or causation. Compare equivalent periods and identify changes in tracking before drawing conclusions. Use only connected/authorized properties.

Blue Bridge handles analytics/tracking setup, measurement integrations and reporting. Check connected evidence before claiming setup is complete or absent; an unavailable tool says nothing about whether tracking runs elsewhere. Assign unverified connections and baselines to Blue Bridge follow-up. Ask the client for a specific external-account approval or business definition only when needed, rather than asking them to choose/run analytics.

When measurements are unavailable to this conversation, propose Blue Bridge’s measurement work: goals, relevant metrics, properties/access to verify, baseline to establish and useful review cadence. Recommend next content/website experiments as hypotheses. Do not claim to connect analytics, schedule recurring reports or save a review when those operations are unavailable. If planning saves are enabled, an explicitly requested measurement plan or evidence summary can be saved as planning Markdown; that does not create measurements or recurring jobs. Return an exportable, clearly labeled proposal.
